Custom Compounding Services

Because every patient is unique, their medication should be too.

Custom Medicine

Personalized Formulations

Compounding is the art and science of preparing custom medications for patients. Our licensed pharmacists work with your physician to formulate medications exactly as you need them—whether that means changing the dosage form, removing an allergen, or adding a flavor.

Compounding Specialties

Clinical Capabilities

Custom formulations prepared to meet your specific clinical and diagnostic requirements.

Dosage Forms

We can convert pills into liquids, topical creams, suppositories, or transdermal gels for patients who have difficulty swallowing oral medications.

Allergy-Friendly

Commercially available medications often contain dyes, gluten, lactose, or preservatives. We can compound the pure active ingredient without the problematic fillers.

Pediatric Flavoring

Children can be sensitive to the taste of medicine. We can compound medications into pleasant-tasting liquids or lozenges in a variety of child-friendly flavors.

Veterinary Care

Pets have unique medication requirements. We compound custom doses, transdermal gels (for the ear), and flavors like tuna or beef for your furry companions.

Hormone Therapy

Customized bioidentical hormone replacement therapy (BHRT) formulated precisely to restore hormonal balance for men and women.

Dermatology Care

Custom compounded topical ointments, creams, and gels targeting eczema, psoriasis, acne, and aesthetic skincare needs.
