Know what to expect before you submit

Answer three questions for a live turnaround estimate — and open any question below for the detail.

Your submission lands in an encrypted queue that only licensed staff can open. A pharmacist verifies the prescriber, checks your profile for interactions and duplicate therapy, then runs your insurance.

If anything needs clarifying we call your prescriber directly — you don't have to chase it. You get an SMS at intake and again the moment your medication is ready.

A compounded preparation is made from raw ingredients rather than pulled off a shelf. The pharmacist selects the base, calculates the strength, prepares it in our sterile lab and then documents a quality check before release.

That process runs 24–48 hours for most formulations. Priority handling can bring it to roughly 24 hours where the formulation allows.

We bill most major commercial plans, Medicare Part D and state Medicaid. Compounded preparations are handled plan-by-plan, so we verify coverage before we begin and tell you the out-of-pocket figure first.

If a medication isn't covered, we look for a therapeutically equivalent option or a discount-card price and walk you through both.

Submissions are transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored under HIPAA-compliant controls. Access is limited to the licensed pharmacists and clinical staff working your request, and every access is logged.

We never sell or share your information for marketing, and delivery packaging carries no indication of its contents.
