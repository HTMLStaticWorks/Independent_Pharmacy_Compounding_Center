Our Mission

To elevate community healthcare by providing specialized compounding, expert clinical guidance, and personalized patient care that large chains cannot match.

Rooted in the Community

Founded in 2005, Mediva started with a simple belief: healthcare is personal. When you walk into our pharmacy, you aren't just a prescription number. You are a neighbor, and our pharmacists take the time to understand your complete health profile.

Over the years, we expanded our services to include a state-of-the-art compounding lab. This allows us to work directly with prescribers to create medications that fit the exact requirements of patients who cannot take mass-produced drugs.

Licensing & Certifications: Fully licensed by the State Board of Pharmacy. PCAB Accredited Compounding Facility.
